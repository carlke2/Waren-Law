# 0025: The home hero is the film only

**Date:** 10 November 2026
**Status:** Accepted

## Decision
The home hero is the film Home already has, full width, with the Nairobi image as the still. The heading, introduction, buttons and three cards sit in the next section. Other pages keep their heroes.

## Why
The home hero should match the full-screen film on pwc.com: muted, looping, with pause and a scroll cue, and no text on the picture.

## Consequences
- Home is the only page that uses the full-screen film height.
- Visitors who prefer reduced motion see the Nairobi still.
