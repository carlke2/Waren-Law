# 0014: Ground rules, frontend reset, research first

**Date:** 4 November 2026
**Status:** Accepted (puts `docs/design/DESIGN-SPEC.md` v1.0 and brief 06 on hold)

## Decision
1. `docs/GROUND-RULES.md` is binding for planner and agent.
2. The existing frontend is deleted completely; backend, data and API stay.
3. Design starts from research of current law firm websites, then site structure, then a new design spec and an approved Home visual. DESIGN-SPEC v1.0 and brief 06 are on hold and will be replaced.
4. Booking moves from Microsoft Bookings/Calendly to a native consultation request form.

## Why
- The owner saw the Next.js development badge and loading behaviour; the site must run independently, in production mode, with complete pages.
- External forms (Microsoft) break the experience and the "native" principle.
- The previous design was rejected; research and an approved visual reduce the risk of a second rejection.
