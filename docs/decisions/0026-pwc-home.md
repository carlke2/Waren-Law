# 0026: The homepage follows a bright editorial flow

**Date:** 10 November 2026
**Status:** Accepted

## Decision
The homepage, under the current header, is a full-width film with no text on it, then a white introduction, three practice cards, one featured session, three insights, client logos, a team preview, a peach close, and a light footer. Gold is the only accent. Team titles on warenlaw.com that differ from the database are updated, and the previous wording is kept in the homepage report.

## Why
The homepage should feel like pwc.com’s flow without using PwC’s logo, photographs, text or orange bar. Waren’s own film, writing, portraits and gold stay. The header is left for a later pass.

## Consequences
- Home no longer shows the Nairobi still, a dark overlay, or the scroll cue.
- The shared footer, cookie notice and WhatsApp button lose their blue fills so the homepage stays bright. Other pages still use their current section colours.
- Four team role lines now match warenlaw.com. No new person was added, because all twenty people on the live team page were already stored.
