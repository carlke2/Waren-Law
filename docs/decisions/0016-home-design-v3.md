# 0016: Home design v3 — Frieden & Forbes layout with Juristo navy and gold

**Date:** 4 November 2026
**Status:** Accepted and LOCKED — 4 Nov 2026 (replaces 0015)

## Decision
1. Home layout follows the Frieden & Forbes reference: hero → welcome → areas of expertise → team band → insights → CTA → footer.
2. The look follows the Juristo reference: deep navy, gold accents, serif headings, dark portrait cards, navy footer with newsletter.
3. WAREN's brand colours (navy `#0B1A4A`, gold `#D4AF37`) are kept; fonts Newsreader + Manrope.
4. The design canvas ("WAREN Law Home Design", copy in `docs/design/reference/home-design-v3.dc.html`) is the visual source; `docs/design/DESIGN-SPEC.md` v3 is its written specification.
5. The hero follows the Apex reference: transparent header over a Nairobi image (video later), no headline, and three compact cards (For Businesses, For Property & Families, For Kenyans Abroad) sitting near the bottom of the image as in the Kemen reference.
6. Testimonials are replaced by latest insights and a recognition strip until the firm approves testimonials.

## Why
Planner's assessment of five references: this combination shows all 8 practice areas and the partners on the home page, reads clearly as a law firm, and keeps the firm's own colours.
